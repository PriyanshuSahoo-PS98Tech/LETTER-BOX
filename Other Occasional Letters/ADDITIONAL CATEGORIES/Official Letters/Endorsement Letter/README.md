# ✅ Endorsement Letter

A confident, authoritative, and professional endorsement letter with animated envelope opening, approval seal-inspired effects, trust-focused styling, and a polished formal recommendation layout.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive endorsement letter features a professional envelope-to-document experience designed to formally support, recommend, approve, or endorse a person, proposal, project, product, service, organization, or initiative. The template includes a trust-focused animated background, approval seal-inspired rotating elements, refined document card layout, professional typography, structured endorsement details, statement of support section, key strengths section, recommendation section, and an emerald-blue-gold theme perfect for professional recommendations, academic endorsements, business endorsements, project approvals, product endorsements, institutional support letters, collaboration recommendations, and official endorsement communication.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open a polished animated envelope and reveal the endorsement letter
- **Approval Seal-Inspired Effects**: Subtle rotating approval rings and professional endorsement particles appear during the opening experience
- **Trust-Focused Background**: Soft trust orbs, glowing dots, grid overlays, and moving approval lines create a reliable professional atmosphere
- **Refined Endorsement Layout**: Professional document-style card suitable for official, academic, business, and institutional endorsements
- **Structured Endorsement Details**: Dedicated section for endorsed name, purpose, endorser, relationship, validity, and supporting basis
- **Statement of Support Section**: Formal area to clearly express confidence and support
- **Key Strengths Section**: Clearly presents reasons, qualities, achievements, and suitability for endorsement
- **Recommendation Section**: Provides a strong closing recommendation for positive consideration
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical endorsement submission
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender name, designation, department, and organization details in the “From” section
   - Update recipient name, authority, designation, department, and organization details in the “To” section
   - Modify the reference number and date according to the official record
   - Customize the subject line according to the person, proposal, project, product, or organization being endorsed
   - Update endorsement details, purpose, relationship, validity, and supporting basis
   - Replace key strengths, recommendation text, and enclosure details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Organization Name" with the actual sender or endorsing authority
   - Mention the exact person, proposal, project, product, service, or organization being endorsed
   - Keep the tone confident, respectful, honest, and professional
   - Clearly state the reason and basis for endorsement
   - Add achievements, performance records, experience, or supporting details if available
   - Mention whether the endorsement is academic, professional, business, official, or institutional
   - Avoid exaggerated claims and keep the endorsement credible and verifiable
