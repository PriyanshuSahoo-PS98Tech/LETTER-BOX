# 📘 Policy Letter

A polished, professional, and compliance-focused policy letter with animated envelope opening, corporate governance styling, structured policy details, and a refined official communication layout.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive policy letter features a professional envelope-to-document experience designed for communicating new policies, revised policies, policy updates, implementation guidelines, compliance requirements, and official organizational instructions. The template includes a corporate animated background, compliance-inspired rotating rings, refined document card layout, professional typography, structured policy details, scope of policy section, key provisions section, implementation guidelines, compliance statement, and a navy-blue-emerald-silver theme perfect for company policies, institutional policies, office circulars, HR policy updates, school or college policy notices, administrative instructions, compliance communications, and formal policy announcements.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open a corporate animated envelope and reveal the policy letter
- **Compliance-Inspired Effects**: Subtle rotating rings and professional policy particles appear during the opening experience
- **Corporate Governance Background**: Soft policy orbs, glowing dots, grid overlays, and moving compliance lines create a formal communication atmosphere
- **Structured Policy Layout**: Professional document-style card suitable for official policy announcements and implementation notices
- **Policy Details Section**: Dedicated section for policy name, number, effective date, issuing department, applicability, and review date
- **Scope of Policy Section**: Formal area to define who the policy applies to and what the policy covers
- **Key Policy Provisions Section**: Clearly presents important rules, conditions, and policy requirements
- **Implementation Guidelines Section**: Provides practical steps for applying the policy effectively
- **Compliance Statement Section**: Communicates expectations regarding responsibility, accountability, and adherence
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical policy letter submission
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender name, organization name, designation, department, and contact details in the “From” section
   - Update recipient name, employees, members, department, branch, or unit details in the “To” section
   - Modify the policy reference number and date according to the official record
   - Customize the subject line according to the policy topic
   - Update the policy name, policy number, effective date, issued by, applicability, and review date
   - Replace scope, provisions, implementation guidelines, compliance statement, and enclosure details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Organization Name" with the actual sender or issuing authority
   - Mention the exact policy name, topic, number, and effective date
   - Keep the tone formal, clear, and authoritative
   - Use simple and direct wording so all recipients can understand the policy requirements
   - Clearly mention who the policy applies to and what actions are expected
   - Add relevant policy documents, checklists, circulars, or previous references if available
   - Avoid ambiguous language because policy letters must communicate rules and expectations clearly

```html
        Address Line<br>
        Email: your.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Recipient Name / Employees / Members</strong><br>
        Department / Branch / Unit<br>

    </p>
```

### Modifying Policy Reference Details

```text
Policy No.: [Mention Policy Reference Number]
Date: [Mention Date]
```

### Modifying Policy Subject

```text
Subject: Communication Regarding [New / Revised] Policy on [Policy Topic]
```

### Customizing Policy Details

```text
Policy Name: [Mention Policy Name]
Policy Number: [Mention Policy Reference Number]
Effective Date: [Mention Effective Date]
Issued By: [Organization / Department Name]
Applicable To: [Employees / Students / Members / Departments]
Review Date: [Mention Review Date, if applicable]
```

### Updating Key Policy Provisions

```text
All concerned persons must follow the guidelines mentioned in this policy.
The policy shall be implemented from the stated effective date.
Any violation or non-compliance may be reviewed as per organizational rules.
Departments must maintain proper records and documentation related to this policy.
Clarifications regarding the policy may be obtained from the concerned authority.
```

### Updating Implementation Guidelines

```text
Read the complete policy document carefully.
Share the policy details with all concerned team members or stakeholders.
Update internal records, procedures, and workflows wherever required.
Ensure timely compliance with all mentioned requirements.
Report any difficulty or clarification request to the responsible department.
```

### Updating Enclosures / References

```text
Full policy document
Implementation guidelines
Compliance checklist
Related circulars or previous policy references
```
