# 📜 Resolution Letter

A formal, authoritative, and professional resolution letter with animated envelope opening, official seal-inspired effects, board-style document formatting, and a polished decision-record presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive resolution letter features a professional envelope-to-document experience designed for recording decisions, approvals, authorizations, appointments, policy adoption, banking approvals, committee decisions, and official actions passed by a board, committee, managing body, society, trust, company, institution, or organization. The template includes an official animated background, seal-inspired rotating elements, refined resolution card layout, professional typography, structured resolution details, resolution passed section, authorized actions section, certification statement section, and a navy-burgundy-gold theme perfect for corporate resolutions, board resolutions, society resolutions, committee resolutions, institutional resolutions, banking resolutions, authorization resolutions, and formal official decision records.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open a formal animated envelope and reveal the resolution letter
- **Official Seal-Inspired Effects**: Subtle rotating seal rings and professional resolution particles appear during the opening experience
- **Board-Style Administrative Background**: Official orbs, glowing dots, grid overlays, and moving gold lines create a formal decision-record atmosphere
- **Polished Resolution Layout**: Professional document-style card suitable for corporate, society, committee, institutional, and official use
- **Structured Resolution Details**: Dedicated section for resolution number, meeting date, meeting type, subject matter, proposer, and seconder
- **Resolution Passed Section**: Formal “RESOLVED THAT” and “FURTHER RESOLVED THAT” wording for official decision recording
- **Authorized Actions Section**: Clearly presents authorized activities, document submission, representation, communication, and implementation steps
- **Certification Statement Section**: Provides a professional certified true copy statement for official validity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical resolution letter submission
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace organization, company, society, trust, or institution details in the “From” section
   - Update recipient, authority, department, bank, or office details in the “To” section
   - Modify the resolution number and date according to the official record
   - Customize the subject line according to the matter, approval, authorization, or decision
   - Update meeting date, meeting type, subject matter, proposer, seconder, and resolution wording
   - Replace authorized actions, certification statement, signatory details, and enclosure details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Organization / Company / Society Name" with the actual legal or official entity name
   - Mention the correct resolution number, meeting date, and venue
   - Use formal “RESOLVED THAT” and “FURTHER RESOLVED THAT” wording for official clarity
   - Clearly identify the authorized person, designation, and scope of authority
   - Add proposer and seconder details where required by organizational procedure
   - Attach meeting minutes, attendance records, or supporting documents if needed
   - Keep the language formal, precise, and suitable for legal, banking, administrative, or institutional use

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Organization Information

```html
<div class="info-box">
    <div class="info-label">From</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Organization / Company / Society Name</strong><br>
        Registered Office Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: official.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Recipient / Authority / Department Name</strong><br>
        Designation / Office Name<br>
        Address Line<br>
        City, State, PIN Code
    </p>
</div>
```

### Modifying Resolution Reference Details

```text
Resolution No.: [Mention Resolution Number]
Date: [Mention Date]
```

### Modifying Resolution Subject

```text
Subject: Resolution Regarding [Mention Matter / Approval / Authorization]
```

### Customizing Resolution Details

```text
Resolution Number: [Mention Resolution Number]
Meeting Date: [Mention Meeting Date]
Meeting Type: [Board / Committee / General Meeting]
Subject Matter: [Mention Matter / Decision]
Proposed By: [Name of Proposer]
Seconded By: [Name of Seconder]
```

### Updating Resolution Wording

```text
RESOLVED THAT [clearly mention the decision, approval, appointment, authorization, opening of account, signing authority, purchase, policy adoption, or any other official matter] be and is hereby approved by the members/board/committee.

FURTHER RESOLVED THAT [Name and Designation of Authorized Person] is hereby authorized to sign, submit, execute, communicate, and complete all necessary documents, forms, declarations, applications, correspondence, and formalities required in connection with the above resolution.
```

### Updating Authorized Actions

```text
To represent the organization/company/society before the concerned authority or institution.
To sign and submit documents, applications, forms, declarations, and supporting papers.
To communicate with banks, offices, departments, agencies, or any relevant authority.
To complete all formalities necessary for implementation of the resolution.
To take any further action required for giving effect to the decision passed.
```

### Updating Enclosures / Supporting Documents

```text
Copy of meeting minutes, if applicable
Attendance record or member list
Authorization proof or identity document
Relevant application, form, or supporting papers
```
