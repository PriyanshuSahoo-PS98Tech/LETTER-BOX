# ☕ Invitation to Coffee Meeting Letter

A warm, elegant, and premium coffee meeting invitation letter with animated envelope opening, cozy café-inspired visuals, and a refined professional document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to coffee meeting letter features a cozy envelope-to-document experience designed to invite someone for a meaningful conversation over coffee. The template includes a warm animated coffee-themed background, elegant invitation card layout, premium typography, structured meeting details, RSVP section, and a rich espresso-caramel theme perfect for professional networking, business discussions, informal meetings, mentorship conversations, collaboration talks, client meetings, and friendly professional invitations.

## ✨ Features

- **Coffee-Themed Envelope Animation**: Click to open a warm animated envelope and reveal the coffee meeting invitation
- **Cozy Café Background Animation**: Floating coffee beans, glowing orbs, and soft steam effects create a welcoming atmosphere
- **Premium Meeting Card Layout**: Elegant document-style presentation suitable for professional and semi-formal invitations
- **Coffee Meeting Details Section**: Dedicated area for date, time, venue, duration, purpose, and meeting type
- **Suggested Discussion Points**: Organized agenda section for a productive coffee conversation
- **RSVP Section**: Dedicated confirmation area for availability and coordination
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical invitation submission
- **Formal Signature Area**: Includes space for sender name, designation, contact, and email details
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the meeting purpose
   - Customize meeting purpose, date, time, venue, duration, and meeting type
   - Update suggested discussion points according to the actual meeting agenda
   - Replace contact information and optional attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Organization Name" with your actual name or organization
   - Mention the exact purpose of the coffee meeting clearly and respectfully
   - Keep the invitation warm, polite, and concise
   - Add flexible timing if the recipient has a busy schedule
   - Mention whether the meeting is formal, informal, networking-based, or collaboration-focused
   - Include café location, map link, calendar invite, or short agenda if required

```text

```

### Customizing Coffee Meeting Details

```text
Meeting Purpose: [Mention Meeting Purpose]
Preferred Date: [Mention Date]
Preferred Time: [Mention Time]
Venue: [Café / Coffee Shop / Office Lounge / Online]
Expected Duration: [Mention Duration, e.g., 30–45 minutes]
```

