# 🪔 Invitation to Festival Letter

A joyful, colorful, and premium festival invitation letter with animated envelope opening, festive lighting effects, celebration confetti, and a refined cultural event-card presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to festival letter features a warm and luxurious envelope-to-invitation experience designed to make the recipient feel welcomed, respected, and excited for the celebration. The template includes a festive animated background, glowing lantern-style elements, rangoli-inspired accents, premium typography, celebration confetti effects, and a traditional saffron-gold-crimson theme perfect for religious festivals, cultural festivals, school celebrations, college festivals, community programs, family festival gatherings, office festive events, and formal festival invitations.

## ✨ Features

- **Elegant Festival Envelope Animation**: Click to open a premium animated envelope and reveal the festival invitation letter
- **Joyful Festival Confetti**: Beautiful festive confetti with celebration colors and decorative symbols
- **Premium Festival Card Layout**: A refined card-style document designed for traditional and modern festival invitations
- **Festive Background Animation**: Floating orbs, glowing lights, animated lanterns, and ribbon effects create a celebration atmosphere
- **Rangoli-Inspired Accents**: Subtle decorative circular patterns add a cultural and festive touch
- **Warm Traditional Styling**: Professional design with saffron, gold, crimson, and festive color harmony
- **Floating Decorative Icons**: Soft festival symbols enhance the invitation experience
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical invitation submission
- **RSVP Section**: Dedicated confirmation area for festival attendance
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender or organization information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the festival name
   - Customize festival name, date, time, venue, host, dress code, and RSVP details
   - Update celebration highlights according to the actual festival program
   - Replace contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Name / Organization Name" with your actual name or organization
   - Update contact information with correct phone number and email address
   - Mention the exact festival being celebrated
   - Include complete venue details for easy guest navigation
   - Add RSVP date for better planning and hospitality arrangements
   - Mention traditional dress code, entry pass, program schedule, or special instructions if required
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the festival is religious, cultural, academic, community-based, family-based, or corporate
