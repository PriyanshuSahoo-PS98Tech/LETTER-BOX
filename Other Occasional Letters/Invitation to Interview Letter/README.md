# 💼 Invitation to Interview Letter

A professional, elegant, and corporate interview invitation letter with animated envelope opening, polished HR styling, and a refined recruitment document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to interview letter features a professional envelope-to-document experience designed for HR departments, recruiters, companies, institutions, and organizations to formally invite shortlisted candidates for an interview. The template includes a corporate animated background, elegant document card layout, premium typography, structured interview details, document checklist, and a navy-blue-emerald theme perfect for job interview invitations, internship interview invitations, campus placement interviews, HR recruitment communication, online interview invitations, and formal candidate correspondence.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open a corporate animated envelope and reveal the interview invitation letter
- **Elegant HR Document Layout**: Clean formal presentation suitable for recruitment and business communication
- **Corporate Background Animation**: Floating orbs, soft dots, and moving grid lines create a professional hiring atmosphere
- **Structured Interview Details**: Dedicated section for position, interview date, time, mode, venue/link, and contact person
- **Documents Checklist Section**: Clearly lists documents candidates should carry or keep ready
- **Confirmation Request Section**: Formal RSVP-style area for candidate availability confirmation
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical interview invitation submission
- **Formal Signature Area**: Includes HR manager/recruiter name, designation, company, and contact details
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company or organization information in the “From” section
   - Update candidate details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the position or job title
   - Customize interview date, time, mode, venue, meeting link, and contact person
   - Update the documents checklist according to company requirements
   - Replace HR contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Company / Organization Name" with the actual company name
   - Replace "Candidate Full Name" with the shortlisted candidate’s name
   - Mention the exact job title or internship position
   - Include complete venue details or online meeting link
   - Add confirmation deadline for better scheduling
   - Mention documents required for verification or selection process
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the interview is corporate, academic, internship-based, campus placement, or internal promotion-related

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Company Information

```html
<div class="info-box">
    <div class="info-label">From</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Company / Organization Name</strong><br>
        HR Department / Recruitment Team<br>
        Company Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: hr@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Candidate Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Candidate Full Name</strong><br>
        Candidate Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: candidate.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Modifying Interview Subject

```text
Subject: Invitation to Attend Interview for the Position of [Job Title]
```

### Customizing Interview Details

```text
Position: [Mention Job Title]
Interview Date: [Mention Interview Date]
Interview Time: [Mention Interview Time]
Mode: [In-Person / Online / Telephonic]
Venue / Link: [Office Address / Meeting Link]
Contact Person: [HR Name and Contact Details]
```

### Updating Documents Checklist

```text
Updated resume or curriculum vitae
Educational certificates and mark sheets
Experience letters or previous employment proof, if applicable
Government-issued identity proof
Portfolio, project samples, or work references, if required
Any other document mentioned by the HR department
```

### Updating Confirmation Request

```text
Please confirm your availability on or before [Mention Confirmation Date].
Contact: [HR Phone Number]
Email: [HR Email Address]
```
