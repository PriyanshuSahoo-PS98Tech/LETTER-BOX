# 🍽️ Invitation to Luncheon Letter

A warm, elegant, and premium luncheon invitation letter with animated envelope opening, graceful dining-themed effects, and a refined hospitality-focused document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to luncheon letter features a sophisticated envelope-to-document experience designed to make the recipient feel warmly welcomed and respectfully invited. The template includes a soft animated luncheon background, elegant invitation card layout, premium typography, warm petal effects, dining-themed visual elements, and an espresso-gold-cream theme perfect for business luncheons, formal lunch gatherings, family luncheons, office lunches, appreciation meals, networking luncheons, farewell lunches, welcome lunches, and social hospitality invitations.

## ✨ Features

- **Elegant Envelope Animation**: Click to open a premium animated envelope and reveal the luncheon invitation letter
- **Warm Petal Celebration**: Soft falling petals appear when the invitation is opened
- **Premium Luncheon Card Layout**: A refined card-style document designed for formal and friendly luncheon invitations
- **Hospitality-Themed Background Animation**: Floating orbs, sparkles, and steam-like effects create a warm dining atmosphere
- **Elegant Dining Styling**: Professional design with a welcoming luncheon mood
- **Floating Dining Icons**: Subtle food, coffee, cake, and hospitality symbols enhance the invitation experience
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical invitation submission
- **RSVP Section**: Dedicated confirmation area for luncheon attendance
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender or organization information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the luncheon date or occasion
   - Customize luncheon occasion, date, time, venue, host, dress code, and RSVP details
   - Update luncheon highlights according to the actual meal or gathering plan
   - Replace contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Name / Organization Name" with your actual name or organization
   - Update contact information with correct phone number and email address
   - Mention the exact purpose of the luncheon
   - Include complete venue or restaurant details for easy guest navigation
   - Add RSVP date for better meal and seating arrangements
   - Mention dress code, menu note, seating arrangement, or special instructions if required
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the luncheon is formal, business-related, family-based, academic, or social

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Sender Information

```html
<div class="info-box">
    <div class="info-label">From</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Your Name / Organization Name</strong><br>
        Your Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: your.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Respected Guest / Recipient Name</strong><br>
        Designation / Organization Name<br>
        Address Line<br>
        City, State, PIN Code
    </p>
</div>
```

### Modifying Luncheon Subject

```text
Subject: Invitation to Attend Luncheon on [Mention Date]
```

### Customizing Luncheon Details

```text
Occasion: [Mention Luncheon Occasion / Purpose]
Date: [Mention Luncheon Date]
Time: [Mention Luncheon Time]
Venue: [Mention Venue / Restaurant / Hall]
Host: [Mention Host Name]
Dress Code: [Formal / Semi-Formal / Casual, if any]
```

### Updating Luncheon Highlights

```text
Warm welcome and guest interaction
Formal or friendly luncheon gathering
Delightful meal and refreshments
Conversation, networking, or celebration moments
Closing greetings and appreciation
```

### Updating RSVP Details

```text
Kindly confirm your presence on or before [Mention RSVP Date].
Contact: [Phone Number]
Email: [Email Address]
```
