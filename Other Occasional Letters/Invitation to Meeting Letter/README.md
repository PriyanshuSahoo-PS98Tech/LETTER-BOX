# 📅 Invitation to Meeting Letter

A professional, elegant, and executive-style meeting invitation letter with animated envelope opening, polished meeting details, and refined formal document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to meeting letter features a professional envelope-to-document experience designed to formally invite participants, colleagues, officials, clients, committee members, or stakeholders to attend an important meeting. The template includes a modern executive background, calm corporate animations, structured meeting details, proposed agenda section, confirmation area, and a navy-blue-teal theme perfect for office meetings, business discussions, academic meetings, committee meetings, project reviews, client meetings, board meetings, departmental meetings, and official professional communication.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open an executive animated envelope and reveal the meeting invitation letter
- **Executive Formal Layout**: Clean document-style presentation suitable for official and corporate communication
- **Corporate Background Animation**: Floating orbs, moving soft lines, and subtle glowing elements create a refined business feel
- **Structured Meeting Details**: Dedicated section for topic, date, time, venue, chairperson, and expected duration
- **Proposed Agenda Section**: Clearly presents discussion points and meeting flow
- **Confirmation / RSVP Section**: Formal area for availability confirmation and contact details
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical meeting invitation submission
- **Formal Signature Area**: Includes space for sender name, designation, organization, and contact details
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender or organization information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the meeting purpose or topic
   - Customize meeting topic, date, time, venue/platform, chairperson, and duration
   - Update the proposed agenda according to the actual discussion points
   - Replace contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Name / Organization Name" with your actual name or organization
   - Update contact information with correct phone number and email address
   - Mention the exact meeting topic and purpose clearly
   - Include complete venue details or online meeting platform information
   - Add confirmation date for better meeting planning
   - Mention attached documents such as agenda, previous minutes, reports, or meeting link
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the meeting is corporate, academic, official, departmental, client-based, or committee-related

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Sender Information

```html
<div class="info-box">
    <div class="info-label">From</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Your Name / Organization Name</strong><br>
        Your Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: your.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Recipient Name / Designation</strong><br>
        Department / Organization Name<br>
        Address Line<br>
        City, State, PIN Code
    </p>
</div>
```

### Modifying Meeting Subject

```text
Subject: Invitation to Attend Meeting Regarding [Meeting Purpose / Topic]
```

### Customizing Meeting Details

```text
Meeting Topic: [Mention Meeting Topic]
Date: [Mention Meeting Date]
Time: [Mention Start Time]
Venue / Platform: [Meeting Room / Online Platform / Location]
Meeting Chair / Host: [Mention Name / Designation]
Expected Duration: [Mention Duration]
```

### Updating Proposed Agenda

```text
Opening remarks and introduction of meeting purpose
Review of previous updates, progress, or relevant background
Discussion of main agenda points and important issues
Suggestions, feedback, and inputs from participants
Decision-making, action points, and responsibility allocation
Closing remarks and next steps
```

### Updating Confirmation Details

```text
Kindly confirm your availability on or before [Mention Confirmation Date].
Contact Person: [Contact Person Name]
Contact: [Phone Number]
Email: [Email Address]
```
