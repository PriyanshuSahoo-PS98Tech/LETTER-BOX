# 📚 Invitation to Study Group Letter

A friendly, elegant, and academic invitation letter with animated envelope opening, collaborative study visuals, and a refined learning-focused document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to study group letter features a professional academic envelope-to-document experience designed to invite classmates, friends, students, or group members to join a study group. The template includes a motivating animated background, elegant study-card layout, professional typography, structured study group details, and a blue-emerald academic theme perfect for exam preparation groups, subject study groups, college study circles, school revision groups, online study sessions, peer learning groups, and collaborative academic planning.

## ✨ Features

- **Academic Envelope Animation**: Click to open a polished animated envelope and reveal the study group invitation letter
- **Friendly Study-Focused Layout**: Clean document-style presentation suitable for students, classmates, and academic groups
- **Motivating Background Animation**: Floating academic symbols, glowing orbs, and moving light lines create a productive learning feel
- **Structured Study Group Details**: Dedicated section for group name, subject, date, time, venue, platform, and coordinator
- **Collaborative Benefits Section**: Clearly explains what members will do together in the study group
- **Confirmation Section**: Dedicated response area for joining confirmation and contact details
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical or academic submission
- **Formal Signature Area**: Includes space for name, class, course, student ID, and contact details
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the study group purpose
   - Customize subject, course, exam name, start date, time, venue, platform, and coordinator details
   - Update study activities and benefits according to the actual group plan
   - Replace contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name" with your actual name
   - Mention the exact subject, course, or exam for the study group
   - Include complete venue or online platform details
   - Add confirmation date for better group planning
   - Mention required books, notes, resources, or study materials if needed
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the invitation is for school, college, coaching, online learning, or exam preparation

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Dear Friend / Classmate / Student Name</strong><br>
        Class / Course / Department<br>
```

### Modifying Study Group Subject

```text
Subject: Invitation to Join Our Study Group
```

### Customizing Study Group Details

```text
Group Name: [Mention Study Group Name]
Subject / Course: [Mention Subject / Course / Exam]
Start Date: [Mention Starting Date]
Time: [Mention Study Time]
Venue / Platform: [Classroom / Library / Online Platform]
```
