# 🏢 Invitation to Trade Show Letter

A professional, modern, and premium trade show invitation letter with animated envelope opening, expo-inspired background effects, and a refined business document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to trade show letter features a premium business expo-style envelope-to-document experience designed for inviting clients, companies, buyers, suppliers, partners, exhibitors, and industry professionals to a trade show, business expo, product exhibition, or commercial event. The template includes a modern animated background, elegant trade show document layout, professional typography, structured event details, exhibitor highlights, and a navy-cyan-gold theme perfect for trade shows, exhibitions, expos, business fairs, product launches, networking events, and industry conferences.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open a premium animated envelope and reveal the trade show invitation letter
- **Expo-Themed Visual Design**: Modern business-inspired design suitable for trade shows and exhibitions
- **Business Networking Feel**: Professional layout that highlights collaboration, partnerships, and commercial opportunities
- **Animated Expo Background**: Floating orbs, scanning lines, and glowing dots create a modern trade-show atmosphere
- **Structured Trade Show Details**: Dedicated section for event name, date, time, venue, booth number, and registration details
- **Trade Show Highlights Section**: Clearly displays product demos, networking, exhibitor booths, and business opportunities
- **Registration / RSVP Section**: Formal confirmation area for attendance, registration, and booth visit scheduling
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical invitation submission
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company or organization information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient company, designation, and address details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the trade show, expo, or exhibition name
   - Customize event name, date, time, venue, booth number, and registration details
   - Update trade show highlights according to the actual business event
   - Replace RSVP, contact information, registration link, and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Company / Organization Name" with your actual company or event organizer name
   - Mention the exact trade show, expo, or exhibition title
   - Include booth or stall number if your company is exhibiting
   - Add registration link or entry pass details for easy attendance
   - Mention product demonstrations, launches, or special meetings if planned
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the invitation is for clients, buyers, suppliers, exhibitors, partners, or industry professionals

```html

    <p>
```

