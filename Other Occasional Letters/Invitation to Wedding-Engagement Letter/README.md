# 💍 Invitation to Wedding/Engagement Letter

A royal, elegant, and joyful wedding or engagement invitation letter with animated envelope opening, floral celebration effects, and a refined ceremonial card presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invitation to wedding/engagement letter features a luxurious envelope-to-invitation experience designed to make guests feel honored, welcomed, and emotionally connected to the celebration. The template includes a royal floral animated background, elegant couple-name section, premium ceremonial card layout, celebratory confetti effects, and a maroon-gold-blush theme perfect for wedding invitations, engagement invitations, reception invitations, family ceremony invitations, traditional functions, and formal social celebrations.

## ✨ Features

- **Royal Envelope Animation**: Click to open a premium wedding-style envelope and reveal the invitation letter
- **Celebration Confetti Effects**: Beautiful confetti and petal-inspired effects appear when the invitation is opened
- **Elegant Couple Name Section**: Dedicated area to display the names of the bride and groom or engaged couple
- **Premium Ceremony Card Layout**: A refined invitation-card presentation designed for wedding and engagement occasions
- **Floral Animated Background**: Floating petals, sparkles, glowing orbs, and golden ribbons create a joyful ceremonial feel
- **Ceremony Details Section**: Organized area for occasion, couple name, date, time, venue, and reception details
- **Program Highlights Section**: Includes rituals, blessings, photography, and hospitality details
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical invitation submission
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Accessible opening through mouse click, Enter key, or Space key

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file as `index.html` and open it in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace host or family information in the “From” section
   - Update recipient details in the “To” section
   - Modify the subject line according to the wedding or engagement ceremony
   - Customize bride/groom or couple names in the couple-name section
   - Update ceremony date, time, venue, reception, dinner, and RSVP details
   - Replace contact information and attachment details as required

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "[Bride/Groom Name]" and "[Bride/Groom Name]" with the actual couple names
   - Mention whether the occasion is a wedding, engagement, reception, ring ceremony, or related function
   - Include complete venue details for easy guest navigation
   - Add RSVP date for better ceremony arrangements
   - Mention reception, dinner, dress code, accommodation, or travel details if required
   - Customize the tone depending on whether the event is traditional, modern, religious, family-based, or formal

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Host Information

```html
<div class="info-box">
    <div class="info-label">From</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Your Family / Host Name</strong><br>
        Your Address<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: your.email@example.com<br>
        Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Recipient Information

```html
<div class="info-box right">
    <div class="info-label">To</div>
    <p>
        <strong>Respected Guest / Recipient Name</strong><br>
        Address Line<br>
        City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Contact Details, if required
    </p>
</div>
```

### Updating Couple Names

```html
<div class="couple-names">
    <div class="label">Celebrating the union of</div>
    <div class="names">[Bride/Groom Name]</div>
    <div class="connector">&</div>
    <div class="names">[Bride/Groom Name]</div>
</div>
```

### Modifying Invitation Subject

```text
Subject: Invitation to Attend the Wedding / Engagement Ceremony of [Name] and [Name]
```

### Customizing Ceremony Details

```text
Occasion: [Wedding / Engagement Ceremony]
Couple: [Bride/Groom Name] & [Bride/Groom Name]
Date: [Mention Ceremony Date]
Time: [Mention Start Time]
Venue: [Mention Venue / Marriage Hall / Location]
Reception / Dinner: [Mention Reception or Dinner Time, if applicable]
```

### Updating Program Highlights

```text
Warm welcome of guests and family members
Traditional wedding / engagement rituals
Blessings and greetings for the couple
Photography and memorable moments
Dinner, refreshments, or reception arrangements
```

### Updating RSVP Details

```text
Kindly confirm your presence on or before [Mention RSVP Date].
Contact: [Phone Number]
Email: [Email Address]
```
