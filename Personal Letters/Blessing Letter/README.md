# 🙏 Blessing Letter

A sacred and spiritual blessing letter with divine envelope animation and floating celestial effects.

## ✞ Overview

This interactive blessing letter features a heavenly envelope-to-message experience designed to share divine blessings, spiritual prayers, and sacred wishes. The letter includes floating divine elements, celestial animations, and a sacred golden-blue theme perfect for offering spiritual comfort, religious blessings, and divine guidance to loved ones.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the sacred blessings
- **Floating Divine Effects**: Beautiful sacred symbols and celestial elements throughout the experience
- **Floating Divine Elements**: Crosses, doves, divine light, and angels in the background
- **Sacred Typography**: Divine fonts (Cinzel) that convey spirituality and reverence
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Interactive Response Buttons**: "Deeply Blessed!" and "Grateful Prayer!" options
- **Blessing Sections**: Dedicated areas for grace and spiritual prayers
- **No Back Navigation**: One-way journey for complete spiritual experience
