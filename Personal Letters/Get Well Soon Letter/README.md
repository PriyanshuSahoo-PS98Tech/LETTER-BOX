# 🌱 Get Well Soon Letter

A warm and encouraging get well soon letter with healing envelope animation and therapeutic energy effects.

## 💚 Overview

This interactive get well soon letter features a nurturing envelope-to-message experience designed to offer comfort and encouragement during illness or recovery. The letter includes floating healing elements, gentle energy animations, and a therapeutic theme perfect for sending support and positive vibes to someone who isn't feeling well.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the healing message
- **Healing Energy Effects**: Gentle rising energy particles throughout the experience
- **Floating Therapeutic Elements**: Plant, sun, flower, and butterfly animations in the background
- **Comforting Typography**: Soothing fonts that convey care and encouragement
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Interactive Response Buttons**: "Feeling Better!" and "Thank You for Caring" options
- **Recovery Sections**: Dedicated areas for support and gentle encouragement
- **No Back Navigation**: One-way journey for complete emotional support

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Change the sender's name in the signature section
   - Modify the specific illness or recovery situation
   - Update the encouragement list with relevant reminders
   - Adjust the support offer based on what you can provide

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Rachel" with your actual name
   - Customize the get well message with specific details about their condition
   - Add personal references to their strength or past recoveries
   - Include specific offers of help or support
