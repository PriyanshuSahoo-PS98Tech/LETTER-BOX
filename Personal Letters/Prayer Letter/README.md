# 🙏 Prayer Letter

A sacred and peaceful prayer letter with serene envelope animation and floating spiritual effects.

## 🕊️ Overview

This interactive prayer letter features a reverent envelope-to-message experience designed to share spiritual support, divine blessings, and heartfelt prayers. The letter includes floating spiritual elements, peaceful animations, and a sacred purple-gold theme perfect for offering comfort, blessings, and spiritual encouragement to those in need of prayer and divine guidance.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the sacred prayer
- **Floating Spiritual Effects**: Beautiful sacred icons throughout the experience
- **Floating Spiritual Elements**: Prayer hands, doves, stars, and spiritual symbols in the background
- **Sacred Typography**: Peaceful fonts that convey reverence and spiritual comfort
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Interactive Response Buttons**: "Amen & Grateful!" and "Blessed & Peaceful!" options
- **Prayer Sections**: Dedicated areas for divine blessings and prayer intentions
- **No Back Navigation**: One-way journey for complete spiritual experience

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Change the sender's name in the signature section
   - Modify the prayer content to match your spiritual message
   - Update the prayer intentions list with specific blessings
   - Adjust the spiritual message based on the recipient's needs

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Father Matthew" with your actual name or title
   - Customize the prayer text with specific spiritual guidance
   - Add personal references to the recipient's spiritual journey
   - Include specific prayer requests or blessings they may need
