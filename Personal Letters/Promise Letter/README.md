# 🤝 Promise Letter

A heartfelt and trustworthy promise letter with elegant envelope animation and floating commitment effects.

## 🤞 Overview

This interactive promise letter features a sacred envelope-to-message experience designed to make solemn promises, commitments, and vows. The letter includes floating promise elements, commitment animations, and a trustworthy purple-teal theme perfect for making meaningful promises and establishing bonds of trust and dedication.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the sacred promise
- **Floating Promise Effects**: Beautiful commitment icons throughout the experience
- **Floating Promise Elements**: Handshakes, rings, crossed fingers, and star symbols in the background
- **Trustworthy Typography**: Reliable fonts that convey sincerity and commitment
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Interactive Response Buttons**: "I Trust You!" and "Promise Accepted!" options
- **Commitment Sections**: Dedicated areas for vows and pledged commitments
- **No Back Navigation**: One-way journey for complete promise experience
