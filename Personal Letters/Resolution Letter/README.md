# 🎯 Resolution Letter

A motivational and inspiring resolution letter with elegant envelope animation and dynamic floating determination effects.

## 🌅 Overview

This interactive resolution letter features a transformative envelope-to-message experience designed to express commitment to personal growth, positive change, and new beginnings. The letter includes floating determination elements, motivational animations, and a vibrant purple-teal theme perfect for making resolutions and inspiring commitment to personal transformation.

## ✨ Features

- **Interactive Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the commitment message
- **Dynamic Floating Effects**: Beautiful determination icons with varied movement patterns from different directions
- **Floating Determination Elements**: Targets, stars, rockets, and strength symbols with enhanced animations
- **Motivational Typography**: Inspiring fonts that convey determination and commitment
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Interactive Response Buttons**: "I'm Ready to Change!" and "Feeling Inspired!" options
- **Resolution Sections**: Dedicated areas for commitments and personal goals
- **Enhanced Animation System**: Emojis move from various angles with spiral, burst, and complex patterns
