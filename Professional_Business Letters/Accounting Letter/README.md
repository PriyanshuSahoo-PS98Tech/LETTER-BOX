# 🧾 Accounting Letter

A professional and sophisticated accounting letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive accounting letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal accounting communication, payment reconciliation, account statement requests, invoice clarification, and financial record verification. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated blue-indigo theme perfect for accounts departments, finance teams, auditors, vendors, clients, and business organizations.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal accounting letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate finance environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey accuracy, trust, and financial clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for official accounting documentation
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender/company accounting details in the header section
   - Update receiver details with actual accounts manager or finance officer information
   - Modify the subject line according to the accounting purpose
   - Customize account ID, invoice number, accounting period, and payment reference
   - Replace outstanding amount, company name, and financial details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Company Name" with your actual name or business name
   - Update accounts department contact information with correct professional details
   - Mention invoice numbers, transaction IDs, UTR numbers, or cheque numbers accurately
   - Clearly state the accounting period for which the statement is required
   - Add supporting documents such as invoices, payment receipts, and previous statements
   - Request confirmation of payable, receivable, credit note, or debit note balances

## 💼 Content Structure

The accounting letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/accounts information
- **Formal date** and accounts department addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the accounting purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** requesting statement or reconciliation formally
- **Account details section** highlighting invoice, payment, and balance information
- **Requested accounting information section** listing financial documents and confirmations required
- **Clarification paragraph** requesting mismatch or adjustment details
- **Closing paragraph** requesting prompt response and acknowledgement
- **Formal signature** with designation and contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for invoices, payment proof, and account statements
