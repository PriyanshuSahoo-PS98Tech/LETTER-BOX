# 🎁 Benefits Letter

A professional and sophisticated benefits letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive benefits letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal employee benefits communication and HR documentation. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-violet theme perfect for employee benefits notices, HR benefit explanations, insurance communication, allowance information, workplace policy updates, and official employee support documentation.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal benefits letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for HR and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey trust and clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission and HR records
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace HR department and company information in the header section
   - Update employee name, employee ID, department, and designation
   - Modify the subject line according to the benefits purpose
   - Customize the benefits list based on company policy
   - Replace company name, HR contact details, and attached policy references throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Employee Name" with the actual employee name
   - Update employee ID, department, and designation correctly
   - Mention the exact benefit type such as insurance, allowance, bonus, or leave policy
   - Add eligibility criteria and claim process if required
   - Include HR contact details for clarification
   - Attach benefit policy documents or claim forms if applicable

```text

```

