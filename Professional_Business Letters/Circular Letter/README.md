# 📢 Circular Letter

A professional and sophisticated circular letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive circular letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for official announcements and formal information circulation. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-violet theme perfect for office circulars, school circulars, college circulars, business announcements, department notices, policy updates, and institutional communication.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal circular letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey authority and clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical notice board display
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace organization/institution information in the header section
   - Update circular number, date, and reference details
   - Modify the subject line according to the announcement purpose
   - Customize important details, effective date, and applicable audience
   - Replace authorized signatory and department details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Organization / Institution Name" with your actual organization name
   - Update official address, email, and phone number
   - Mention the correct circular number and issue date
   - Clearly define the circular topic and effective date
   - Specify who the circular applies to
   - Add instructions, deadlines, and contact person details if required

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Organization Information

```html
<div class="organization-info">
    <h2>Organization / Institution Name</h2>
    <p>
        Department / Office Name<br>
        Address Line, City, State, PIN Code<br>
        Email: official.email@example.com | Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
    </p>
</div>
```

### Modifying Circular Subject

```text
Subject: Circular Regarding [Mention Announcement / Notice / Policy]
```

### Customizing Circular Details

```text
Circular No.: [Mention Circular Number]
Effective Date: [Mention Date]
Applicable To: [Employees / Students / Staff / Departments]
Department Responsible: [Mention Department Name]
Contact Person: [Mention Contact Name / Designation]
```

### Print Functionality

Use:

* **Ctrl + P** (Windows)
* **Cmd + P** (Mac)

Print styles automatically optimize the layout for professional circular submission, office records, and notice board display.
