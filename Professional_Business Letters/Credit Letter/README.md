# 💳 Credit Letter

A professional and sophisticated credit letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive credit letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal credit requests and financial communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated blue-cyan theme perfect for requesting credit facilities, business credit, supplier credit, payment extension, bank credit support, and official financial correspondence.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal credit letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for financial and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey trust, responsibility, and clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender/applicant contact information in the header section
   - Update bank/company/credit department details with actual information
   - Modify the subject line according to the credit request type
   - Customize credit amount, purpose, repayment period, and supporting details
   - Replace account number, customer ID, company name, and reference details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Company Name" with your actual name or business name
   - Update contact information with correct professional details
   - Mention the exact credit amount and reason for requesting credit
   - Clearly specify repayment duration and repayment method
   - Add customer ID, account number, invoice number, or quotation reference if applicable
   - Attach financial records, identity proof, previous transaction details, or business documents when required

## 💼 Content Structure

The credit letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/applicant information
- **Formal date** and credit department addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the credit request purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** requesting credit facility formally
- **Credit request details section** highlighting amount, purpose, duration, and repayment method
- **Commitment section** confirming repayment responsibility and agreement to terms
- **Document support paragraph** mentioning verification and required documents
- **Closing paragraph** requesting approval and further instructions
- **Formal signature** with contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for financial, identity, and transaction documents
