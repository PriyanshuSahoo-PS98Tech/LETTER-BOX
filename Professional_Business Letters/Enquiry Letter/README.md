# 📩 Enquiry Letter

A professional and sophisticated enquiry letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive enquiry letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal information requests and professional business communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated green-emerald theme perfect for product enquiries, service enquiries, quotation requests, admission enquiries, business proposals, and official information requests.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal enquiry letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey clarity and trust
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender contact information in the header section
   - Update recipient details with the actual company/institution information
   - Modify the subject line according to the enquiry purpose
   - Customize the requested information section based on your requirement
   - Replace product/service name and organization details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name" with your actual full name
   - Update contact information with your correct details
   - Mention the exact product, service, course, or information you are enquiring about
   - Clearly list the details you want to receive
   - Add quantity, purpose, budget, or deadline if applicable
   - Request brochure, catalogue, quotation, or official details when needed

## 💼 Content Structure

The enquiry letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender information
- **Formal date** and recipient addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the enquiry purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** introducing the enquiry formally
- **Information requested section** highlighting required details
- **Purpose of enquiry section** explaining why the information is needed
- **Request paragraph** asking for quotation, brochure, or official response
- **Closing paragraph** requesting prompt communication
- **Formal signature** with contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for supporting information

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

    <strong>Your Full Name</strong><br>
    Your Address<br>
    City, State, PIN Code<br>
    Email: your.email@example.com<br>

### Modifying Enquiry Subject

```text
Subject: Enquiry Regarding [Product / Service / Course / Information]
```

### Customizing Requested Information

```text
Product/Service Name: [Mention Name]
Required Details: [Price, Availability, Specifications, Terms]
Purpose: [Personal / Business / Academic / Official Use]
```

### Print Functionality

Use:

* **Ctrl + P** (Windows)
* **Cmd + P** (Mac)
