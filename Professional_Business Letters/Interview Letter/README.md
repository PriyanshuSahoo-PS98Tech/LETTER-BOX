# 🧑‍💼 Interview Letter

A professional and sophisticated interview letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive interview letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal interview invitations and professional recruitment communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-purple theme perfect for job interview invitations, HR communications, candidate shortlisting notices, recruitment scheduling, and official hiring process correspondence.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal interview letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey trust and authority
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company/organization information in the header section
   - Update candidate details with actual applicant information
   - Modify the subject line according to the job position
   - Customize interview date, time, venue, meeting link, and contact person
   - Replace HR manager/recruiter name and company details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Company / Organization Name" with the actual company name
   - Replace "Candidate Full Name" with the shortlisted candidate’s name
   - Mention the exact position title and department
   - Add interview mode such as offline, online, telephonic, or panel interview
   - Include venue address or meeting link clearly
   - Mention required documents and confirmation instructions

## 💼 Content Structure

The interview letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete company/HR information
- **Formal date** and candidate addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the interview invitation
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** informing the candidate about shortlisting
- **Interview details section** highlighting position, date, time, mode, and venue
- **Documents required section** listing important documents for the interview
- **Confirmation paragraph** requesting availability confirmation
- **Closing paragraph** wishing the candidate success
- **Formal signature** with HR/recruiter contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for schedule, job description, or meeting instructions

```html
    Human Resources Department<br>
    Company Address<br>
    City, State, PIN Code<br>
    Email: hr@company.com<br>
    Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX

```

### Modifying Interview Subject

```text
Subject: Interview Invitation for the Position of [Job Title]
```

### Customizing Interview Details

```text
Position: [Job Title]
Date of Interview: [Mention Date]
Time: [Mention Time]
Mode: [Offline / Online / Telephonic]
Venue / Meeting Link: [Mention Address or Link]
Contact Person: [HR / Recruiter Name]
```
