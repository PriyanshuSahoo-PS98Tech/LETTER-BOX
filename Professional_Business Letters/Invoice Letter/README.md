# 🧾 Invoice Letter

A professional and sophisticated invoice letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive invoice letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal billing communication and professional payment requests. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated slate-gray theme perfect for invoice submission, service billing, product billing, payment reminders, business transactions, and official financial communication.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal invoice letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey clarity and financial reliability
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for invoice submission and records
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company/business contact information in the header section
   - Update client billing details with actual customer information
   - Modify the invoice number, order reference, due date, and payment status
   - Customize product/service descriptions, quantity, rate, tax, discount, and total amount
   - Replace payment details such as bank account, IFSC code, UPI ID, and payment terms

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Company / Business Name" with your actual business name
   - Update GSTIN, contact number, email, and business address correctly
   - Mention a proper invoice number and order reference number
   - Clearly specify product/service details, quantity, rate, and total payable amount
   - Add payment due date, bank details, UPI ID, and transaction instructions
   - Attach invoice copy, purchase order, agreement, or delivery confirmation if applicable

## 💼 Content Structure

The invoice letter includes:

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Company Information

```html
<div class="company-info">
    <strong>Your Company / Business Name</strong><br>
    Business Address<br>
    City, State, PIN Code<br>
    Email: billing@example.com<br>
    Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX<br>
    GSTIN: [GST Number]
</div>
```

### Modifying Invoice Subject

```text
Subject: Invoice Submission for [Products / Services Provided]
```

### Customizing Invoice Details

```text
Invoice No: [Mention Invoice Number]
Order Ref: [Mention Order Reference]
Due Date: [Mention Due Date]
Description: [Product / Service Description]
Quantity: [Mention Quantity]
Rate: [Mention Rate]
Total Payable: [Mention Grand Total]
```

### Customizing Payment Information

```text
Payment Method: [Bank Transfer / UPI / Cheque / Cash]
Account Holder Name: [Mention Name]
Bank Name: [Mention Bank Name]
Account Number: [Mention Account Number]
IFSC Code: [Mention IFSC Code]
UPI ID: [Mention UPI ID]
```

### Print Functionality

Use:

* **Ctrl + P** (Windows)
