# 🏥 Medical Letter

A professional and sophisticated medical letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive medical letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal medical leave requests and health-related communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated cyan-blue theme perfect for medical leave applications, health documentation, institutional medical requests, workplace medical communication, and official health-related submissions.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal medical letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and institutional environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey clarity and trust
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender/patient contact information in the header section
   - Update recipient details with the actual principal, manager, or authority information
   - Modify the subject line according to the medical purpose
   - Customize leave dates, reason, duration, and medical certificate details
   - Replace institution/company name and identification details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name" with your actual full name
   - Update contact information with your correct details
   - Mention accurate leave start date and end date
   - Add roll number, employee ID, class, department, or designation
   - Attach medical certificate, prescription, or supporting documents
   - Keep the medical reason professional and concise

## 💼 Content Structure

The medical letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/patient information
- **Formal date** and recipient addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the medical leave request
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** introducing the medical reason formally
- **Medical leave details section** highlighting dates, duration, and documents
- **Responsibility paragraph** explaining pending work or duty management
- **Formal request section** asking for approval
- **Closing paragraph** requesting understanding and support
- **Formal signature** with contact and ID details
- **Enclosures list** for medical certificate and supporting documents
