# 💳 Payment Request Letter

A professional and sophisticated payment request letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive payment request letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal payment follow-ups and professional financial communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated blue-indigo theme perfect for pending payment requests, invoice follow-ups, client billing communication, service payment reminders, vendor payment requests, and official accounts department correspondence.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal payment request letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey clarity, trust, and financial seriousness
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender/business contact information in the header section
   - Update client/company/accounts department details with actual information
   - Modify the subject line according to the payment request purpose
   - Customize invoice number, due date, amount, and payment details
   - Replace company name, bank details, and transaction references throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Company Name" with your actual name or business name
   - Update contact information with correct professional details
   - Mention invoice number, invoice date, and due amount clearly
   - Add payment due date and agreed payment terms
   - Include bank details, UPI ID, or payment instructions carefully
   - Attach invoice copy, work completion proof, or delivery confirmation if applicable

## 💼 Content Structure

The payment request letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/business information
- **Formal date** and recipient/accounts department addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the payment request purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** requesting release of pending payment
- **Payment details section** highlighting invoice number, date, amount, and due date
- **Payment information section** providing bank/UPI details
- **Reminder paragraph** requesting prompt action and transaction confirmation
- **Professional relationship paragraph** maintaining respectful business tone
- **Formal signature** with designation and contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for invoice, agreement, or completion proof
