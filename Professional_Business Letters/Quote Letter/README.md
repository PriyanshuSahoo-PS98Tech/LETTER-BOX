# 💼 Quote Letter

A professional and sophisticated quote letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive quote letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal quotation submissions and professional business communication. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-purple theme perfect for product quotations, service quotations, business proposals, client pricing communication, vendor responses, and official quotation sharing.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal quote letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey trust and clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company/sender contact information in the header section
   - Update client/customer details with actual recipient information
   - Modify the subject line according to the quotation purpose
   - Customize product/service descriptions, quantity, rate, and amount
   - Replace quotation reference, validity, delivery timeline, and payment terms

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Company Name" with your actual business or organization name
   - Update contact information with correct official details
   - Mention quotation reference number for tracking
   - Clearly specify product/service description, quantity, rate, and final amount
   - Add taxes, delivery charges, support terms, or warranty details if applicable
   - Request client confirmation or approval before order processing

## 💼 Content Structure

The quote letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete company/sender information
- **Formal date** and client/customer addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the quotation purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** thanking the client for enquiry
- **Quotation details section** highlighting description, quantity, rate, and amount
- **Terms and conditions section** explaining validity, payment, taxes, and delivery
- **Price revision note** for quantity/specification changes
- **Approval request paragraph** asking for confirmation
- **Formal signature** with authorized signatory details
- **Reference/attachment section** for catalogue, quotation copy, and terms
