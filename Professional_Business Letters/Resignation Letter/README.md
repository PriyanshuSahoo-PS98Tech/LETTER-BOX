# 📋 Resignation Letter

A professional and formal resignation letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and sophisticated document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive resignation letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal business resignation notifications. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated red-gray theme perfect for formal resignations, career transitions, and professional departure communications with supervisors and human resources departments.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal resignation notice
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain formal presentation
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey professionalism and respect
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for HR documentation
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response options
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace employee contact information and details in the header section
   - Update supervisor and HR department information
   - Modify the last working day date to provide appropriate notice
   - Customize transition commitments based on your specific responsibilities
   - Replace company name and department details throughout the letter
   - Update employee ID and position title

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name" with your actual name
   - Update contact information with your professional details
   - Specify your exact position title and department
   - Include your actual employee ID number
   - Customize the reason for resignation (optional and professional)
   - Add specific project handover details relevant to your role
   - Update copy recipients based on company hierarchy
