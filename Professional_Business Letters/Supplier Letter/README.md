# 🚚 Supplier Letter

A professional and sophisticated supplier letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive supplier letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal supplier communication and vendor partnership enquiries. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-blue theme perfect for supplier onboarding, vendor communication, quotation requests, procurement enquiries, business partnership discussions, and official supply-related correspondence.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal supplier letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey reliability and business clarity
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace company/sender contact information in the header section
   - Update supplier/vendor details with actual business information
   - Modify the subject line according to the supplier communication purpose
   - Customize product category, expected quantity, delivery location, and payment terms
   - Replace company name and supplier requirement details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Company Name" with your actual name or business name
   - Update contact information with correct professional details
   - Mention the exact product or material category required
   - Clearly specify supply quantity, delivery location, and business terms
   - Request catalogue, quotation, GST details, company profile, and payment terms
   - Add business requirement summary or product list as attachment if needed

## 💼 Content Structure

The supplier letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/company information
- **Formal date** and supplier/vendor addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the supplier communication purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** introducing the supplier request formally
- **Supplier information requested section** highlighting required details
- **Business terms section** covering quantity, delivery, payment, and quotation validity
- **Verification paragraph** requesting company profile and registration details
- **Response request paragraph** asking for catalogue, quotation, and terms
- **Formal signature** with designation and contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for product list and business requirement summary
