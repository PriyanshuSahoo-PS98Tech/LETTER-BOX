# 🤝 Vendor Letter

A professional and sophisticated vendor letter with enterprise-grade envelope animation and refined document presentation.

## 🏢 Overview

This interactive vendor letter features a corporate-standard envelope-to-document experience designed for formal vendor communication and business partnership correspondence. The letter includes subtle professional animations, enterprise-grade styling, and a sophisticated indigo-blue theme perfect for vendor registration, supplier communication, procurement coordination, business partnership requests, vendor verification, and official vendor management communication.

## ✨ Features

- **Professional Envelope Animation**: Click to open the envelope and reveal the formal vendor letter
- **Enterprise-Grade Styling**: Sophisticated design suitable for official and corporate environments
- **Floating Professional Elements**: Subtle background animations that maintain professionalism
- **Corporate Typography**: Professional fonts that convey trust and business reliability
- **Responsive Design**: Works perfectly on all devices (desktop, tablet, mobile)
- **Print-Optimized**: Professional print styles for physical submission
- **No Interactive Elements**: Pure document presentation without casual response buttons
- **Keyboard Navigation**: Professional accessibility features for enterprise use

## 🚀 Usage Instructions

1. **Setup**: Save the HTML file and open in any modern web browser
2. **Customization**: Edit the content in the HTML file:
   - Replace sender/company contact information in the header section
   - Update vendor details with actual vendor or supplier information
   - Modify the subject line according to the vendor communication purpose
   - Customize vendor requirements, business terms, and documentation details
   - Replace company name and reference details throughout the letter

3. **Personalization Tips**:
   - Replace "Your Full Name / Company Name" with your actual name or business name
   - Update contact information with correct professional details
   - Mention vendor registration number, quotation reference, or previous communication if applicable
   - Clearly specify required documents such as GST, PAN, catalogue, and pricing details
   - Add payment terms, delivery expectations, and quality requirements
   - Request acknowledgement and official confirmation from the vendor

## 💼 Content Structure

The vendor letter includes:
- **Professional header** with complete sender/company information
- **Formal date** and vendor addressing
- **Clear subject line** specifying the vendor communication purpose
- **Professional salutation** with proper business etiquette
- **Opening paragraph** introducing vendor communication formally
- **Vendor requirements section** highlighting required documents and details
- **Business terms section** explaining supply, payment, and delivery expectations
- **Coordination paragraph** requesting timely updates and professional compliance
- **Closing paragraph** requesting acknowledgement and cooperation
- **Formal signature** with designation and contact details
- **Reference/attachment section** for vendor registration or quotation details

## 🛠️ Customization Guide

### Updating Sender Information

```html
<div class="sender-info">
    <strong>Your Full Name / Company Name</strong><br>
    Your Business Address<br>
    City, State, PIN Code<br>
    Email: your.email@example.com<br>
    Phone: +91 XXXXX XXXXX
</div>
```

### Modifying Vendor Subject

```text
Subject: Vendor Communication Regarding [Purpose / Registration / Partnership]
```

### Customizing Vendor Details

```text
Vendor Name: [Mention Vendor Company Name]
Required Documents: [GST, PAN, Catalogue, Pricing, Registration Details]
Business Terms: [Payment Terms, Delivery Timeline, Quality Policy]
Reference Number: [Quotation / Vendor ID / Previous Communication]
```

### Print Functionality

Use:

* **Ctrl + P** (Windows)
* **Cmd + P** (Mac)

Print styles automatically optimize the layout for professional vendor submission.
